# Fuse 0.2.2 - The Insignificant Update

## New

- **Addons' tabs fold away as you scroll.** Scroll down in Cartridge or the Store and the
  Cartridge and Store tabs shrink, with a short animation, into a small pair of icons at the top
  left, leaving the room to the page. Back at the top, or pressing Up into them, they open again.

## Changed

- The Store's first page (choosing Standard or Dual-Screen) now fits any screen: the two editions
  share the room left under the words, the words get smaller on short screens, and the cards have
  clear space between them. "Recommended here" now sits beside the edition's name.

## Fixed

- On 6-inch handhelds the Store's first page ran off the bottom of the screen, and scrolling it
  slid the page under the tabs and the top line.
- After an app was installed from the Store for the first time, the Store could say it was updated.
