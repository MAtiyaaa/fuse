# Fuse 0.0.2 - The Polish Update

## New

Fuse's first round of fixes from real hands-on time on a dual-screen Android handheld. Thank you for
the reports, and keep them coming.

- **Achievements tab.** Your RetroAchievements profile, recent unlocks, games in progress and mastered
  sets in their own tab. Anything matched to a game in your library opens that game.
- **A look for every system.** Systems get their logo, an artwork panel and their brand colour from
  the [Art Book Next](https://github.com/anthonycaccese/art-book-next-es-de) pack (CC BY-NC-SA 2.0).
  Fuse downloads the art when a system has none and never bundles it. Pick the Classic, Outline,
  Noir, Circuit or Screenshots style, or download art for every system again, in Settings, Systems.
  A system's Media screen offers every style and the logo, and an icon you choose yourself always
  wins.
- **Detect my buttons.** Press the right face button, then your confirm button, and Fuse sets the
  layout (Xbox, Nintendo or PlayStation) and which button confirms. In Settings, Inputs, in the
  controls screen and during setup.
- **Paste** on the on-screen keyboard, R to paste from anywhere on it, and Ctrl+V on a hardware
  keyboard. API keys no longer have to be typed by hand.
- **Missing, Hidden and Removed** views in the Library, shown while they have games. Scan for a
  missing game again or let Fuse forget it; bring hidden or removed games back. Files are never
  touched.
- **Remove from Continue Playing.** A game leaves the shelf until you play it again.
- **Arrange your systems.** Hold confirm on a system on Home or in Systems and move it with the
  D-pad. The order is used everywhere.
- **Key checks.** Saving a SteamGridDB, IGDB or TheGamesDB key tests it with a real request, and
  Settings, Media and Scraping shows Working, Key rejected or Offline. A Test keys row runs the
  checks again.
- **Second screen as Home.** With Fuse as your Home app, Fuse is also the second screen's Home.
- **Games with two screens.** DS, DSi, 3DS and Wii U games get the second screen: the companion steps
  aside while they run and comes back with Fuse.
- **Second screen status** in Settings, Displays, with the second screen's recent events, and the
  **last crash report** in Settings, About, to make device problems easier to report.

Downloads: `Fuse-0.0.2-android.apk` (Android 9 or newer), `Fuse-0.0.2-x86_64.AppImage` (64-bit x86
Linux) and `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Changed

- **Top bar.** Every tab shows its name when there is room, and Search and Settings have their own
  buttons at the end, reachable by touch or by moving right past the last tab. Tabs are Home,
  Systems, Library, Achievements and Apps; Cartridge shows while Cartridge is installed. A tab order
  you never changed moves to the new order.
- **Library header.** The row with every console is gone. The header now has a few views, the game
  count, and System, Sort and View buttons. System narrows the library to one system, and your sort
  is remembered. Recently played only lists games you played.
- **Clean names by default.** "Metroid Fusion (USA) (Rev 1) [!]" shows as "Metroid Fusion". Existing
  games are cleaned once (undo in Settings, Library), and custom titles are never touched. List
  numbers ("0123 - "), bare versions ("v1.1") and re-release tags such as "(Virtual Console)" are
  now removed too, and a confident match fills in a game's proper name.
- **Button layout.** The layout (Xbox, Nintendo or PlayStation) now only sets how the buttons are
  labelled and where hints show confirm. A separate "Swap confirm and back" swaps the keys, for pads
  that report buttons by position. The old Nintendo toggle becomes Nintendo labels without a swap.
- **Media on the game page.** Media is second on the game page and right after Play in the options.
  Find Details and Art now fills what is missing for that game instead of only opening the Media
  screen.
- **Performance profiles.** The refresh rate follows the profile on Android (the fastest mode on
  High, 60 Hz on Balanced and Low Power). Background art is decoded no larger than the profile
  allows, glass panels frost the art behind them, and art for the next tiles is fetched ahead, fewer
  in Low Power. Low Power also shrinks the image cache. Settings and the Quick Menu say what the
  current profile does.
- **Apps.** A tap opens an app straight away.
- **Hints under the keyboard** use your pad's own button glyphs.

## Fixed

- Choosing the Nintendo layout on a handheld with Nintendo buttons (such as AYN devices) swapped
  confirm and back a second time.
- Pressing a button during button mapping or the controller test could leave the screen, because
  the system Back button skipped them.
- Find icon could close Fuse. Failures while searching for art now show a message, the "Which game
  is this?" list no longer breaks when two sources share a game number, and a key pasted with a line
  break or invisible characters no longer breaks requests.
- The second-screen companion opened and closed straight away when the second screen briefly
  reported another power state, and did not come back in front after a game or another app.
- Emulators for DS and 3DS could not use the second screen while the companion was on it.
- Shelves above the selection on Home were invisible when scrolled to by touch, and Home could be
  scrolled far past its content.
- Home shelves, the Apps grid after switching between Pinned and All apps, and the Quick Menu did
  not follow the stick.
- Apps ignored taps on Pinned and All apps and needed two taps to open an app, and the focused tab
  was not visible.
- Settings could only be reached through the Quick Menu.
- Several performance settings had no effect.
- Continue Playing's two-week window did not move while Fuse stayed open.
- "Arrange Home" in a shelf's options did nothing.
