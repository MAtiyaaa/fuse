# Fuse 0.1.6 - The Patch & Widget Update

## New

- **Widgets you can resize.** Channels is now a board of widgets, like a phone's home screen. Every
  widget can be one to four cells across and one to three down, and each of the 19 kinds is
  designed for every shape: a list of games shows its first game's art with the next covers beside
  it, the clock draws a dial once it has room, storage becomes a ring, and so on.
  - Hold a widget to arrange the board. Drag a widget to move it while the others spring out of its
    way, drag its corner to resize it cell by cell (with a tick for each), tap the badge to take it
    off, or add one from the Add tile.
  - With a controller, hold A to pick a widget up and move it with the D-pad, or use Options,
    Resize and the D-pad. B finishes.
  - Settings, Home lists the board's widgets with their sizes, adds them, and resets the board.
- **Flow is rows.** Flow keeps to rows of games, systems, apps and collections; clock, storage,
  playtime and the other widgets live on the board. Most played can be a row of its own.
- **A real reorder sheet.** Section order, source order and Flow's row order open a list with grips:
  drag a grip, or press A to pick a row up and move it with the D-pad.
- **Storage in two panes.** Drives and every system with its share of the space on the left, the
  games on the right, each scrolling on its own. Left goes to the systems, A on one shows only its
  games, and A again shows them all.
- **Developer options.** Tap the version in About five times to turn them on until Fuse closes:
  replay setup without changing anything, skip required setup steps, and a live frame-time graph.
- The right stick opens the quick menu, along with Start and M.

## Changed

- Much smoother. Box art behind the interface is blurred once instead of every frame, the room no
  longer fades at every step of a held direction, menus and Settings stop rebuilding their rows
  while you scroll, quick runs along the top bar switch pages without stacking animations, and
  Systems no longer paints a layer per card. On the desktop test rig, frames cost about 35 to 40%
  less on Systems, the Library and runs across the top bar, and about 15% less in a game's
  options. Settings is about 10% lighter, and Storage costs about the same while showing two panes.
- The top bar's underline keeps up with fast runs.
- System tiles show only the system's logo.
- On the on-screen keyboard, a tap flashes the key and lets go, and the controller's focus looks
  different from a press.

## Fixed

- The Storage widget opened Achievements.
- Tapping a row in a game's options briefly highlighted the row above it, and the list jumped.
- On a game's page, the D-pad couldn't reach Play history or On this device.
- The theme preview used your games' art as its background.
- Icons ran into the words on the second screen's third page.
- Search showed a system's short name instead of its logo.
