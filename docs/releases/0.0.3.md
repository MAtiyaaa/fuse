# Fuse 0.0.3 - The Sound and Screens Update

## New

The second round of fixes from hands-on time on a dual-screen Android handheld. Thank you for the
reports, and keep them coming.

- **Menu music.** Settings has a new Sound section. Choose a song on your device (MP3 works on
  Android and Linux) and it loops quietly in Fuse's menus. Fuse keeps its own copy of the file. Menu
  music has its own on/off switch and volume, 20% to start. The music fades out when a game starts or
  Fuse goes to the background, and comes back when you do. Fuse does not come with a song.
- **Sound effects volume.** Interface sounds have their own volume, separate from the music. It is in
  the new Sound section; it used to be "Sound volume" in Inputs.
- **Search as.** At the top of Manage media for a game, set the name Fuse searches for art and details
  with. Use it when a file's name is nothing like the game's ("pkmn_fr_final"). It replaces the file
  name in searches too. Typing the game's title, or nothing, goes back to searching by title.
- **Identify game.** Also in Manage media: lists every match SteamGridDB, IGDB and TheGamesDB have for
  that name, with where it comes from, its system and year. Pick the right one and Fuse uses its
  name, details and art. Nothing changes until you pick, and art you chose yourself stays.
- **Switch Home style in one press.** The Quick Menu has a Home tile that flips between Flow and
  Channels. The options on Flow's shelves and on Channels also offer "Switch to Channels" (or Flow)
  and Home settings.

Downloads: `Fuse-0.0.3-android.apk` (Android 9 or newer), `Fuse-0.0.3-x86_64.AppImage` (64-bit x86
Linux) and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. 0.0.3 installs over 0.0.2 and keeps your settings.

## Changed

- **Systems.** Smaller cards, six across on a handheld instead of three, under a slim header with the
  system's logo or name, its game count and its emulator. The focused system's art stands on the
  right as the background. BIOS and emulator counts are no longer shown on the grid; they are on the
  system's page and in its settings. A card is only marked when no emulator is installed.
- **Second screen.** While Fuse is in front, the companion is shown by Fuse's own window, the way
  Cartridge does it. Before, Fuse opened a separate app window on the second screen. The companion
  goes away while a game or another app is in front, and comes back with Fuse. Games that use both
  screens (DS, DSi, 3DS, Wii U) keep the second screen to themselves. The second screen settings now
  show on Android 9 too.
- **Arranging.** Tap anywhere outside the tiles, or on a tile, to put down a shelf, system or channel
  you are moving. Items slide into their new places, and the one you are carrying floats a little
  above the rest.
- **Selected tile glow.** The glow behind the selected tile is smaller and darker, so it no longer
  spreads orange over the row below.
- **Quick Menu.** The Search tile became the Home tile. Search is the button in the top line (or Y).
- **Channels.** Options opens a menu (move the channel, switch to Flow, Home settings) instead of
  jumping straight into Settings.

## Fixed

- Manage media never found art on Android. It kept loading, then said "Something went wrong
  (NoClassDefFoundError)". Find icon failed the same way, and titles were never matched on the device.
  One pattern in Fuse's title matching could not be read by Android. Every search now works, and a
  search that fails shows a message instead of loading forever.
- The second screen stayed empty on AYN handhelds.
- Moving through the top line with the stick squeezed the clock and status icons, because the LB and
  RB hints took their room.
