# Fuse 0.2.3 - The Other Fix Update

## New

- **A guided Theme Studio.** Make your own theme step by step: the room (light or dark, room and
  panels), text, accent, focus and signals, shape, background, effects, motion and sound, then save.
  Every colour opens into hue, saturation and lightness or takes a colour code, says how well it
  reads with its contrast ratio, and offers a fix when it's hard to read. The shoulder buttons move
  between steps.
- **Paper Mint** is now a built-in theme: a bright desk with a cool green accent.
- **Second screen in the quick menu.** With a second screen connected, a Second screen tile turns
  it Off, On (the selected game) or Companion (beside the game you're playing).
- **Every Store app is supported.** Pico8 Android (a new PICO-8 system) and Swiff (a new Flash
  system) start your games; Gopher64 and X360 Mobile open the app, since they don't let other apps
  start games; Winlator-Ludashi works like Winlator Cmod. Each app's page says what Fuse does with it.
- **Streaming and Tools in Apps.** Moonlight and Artemis list under Streaming, frontends and
  emulation helpers under Tools.
- **Store icons.** Apps you haven't installed yet show the icon they publish themselves.
- **Reset Name and Details** in a game's options, for a game mixed up with another, and **Check
  RomM matches again** in Settings, Cartridge.
- **Restart Store setup** in the developer options.

## Changed

- The Store follows your theme's accent: no more green. Its header is a clear title with quiet
  facts beside it, and the Standard and Dual-Screen drawings are redrawn with Fuse lit on their
  screens.
- Glass, Crossbar, Orbital, Wave, Blades, Channels, CRT, Starlight, Pitch, Blossom, Lagoon, Canopy,
  Opal and Ridge have richer colours, their own signal colours and quieter text that still reads well.
- New collection is now a button at the end of the Collections header instead of a dashed card, with
  a proper page when you have none yet.
- RomM's covers, logos and screenshots are kept in Fuse's own folder.

## Fixed

- Games could take another game's name from RomM (Pokemon Red shown as Ratchet & Clank, Cooking Mama
  as Pokemon Red). RomM's details are now only taken when RomM's system and name match the game, and
  games mixed up before are put back by themselves.
- RomM logos and screenshots showed "by RomM" but stayed blank.
- On Linux, a game's own folders (and an emulator's saves and caches) were counted as games, making
  thousands of them. The next scan reads every folder again and clears them away.
- In light themes the art behind the interface turned into a grey haze that made text hard to read.
- Cartridge's Now downloading card cut off the sizes and the system's name.
