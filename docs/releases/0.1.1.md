# Fuse 0.1.1 - The Second Screen Update

## New

The bottom screen of a dual-screen handheld, redesigned from top to bottom, plus touch where it was
missing and two Android fixes from hands-on time on the AYN Thor. Thank you for every report.

- **The bottom screen, redesigned.** Every page fits the screen without scrolling, under a top line
  with the page's title and the status, and above the page dots.
  - **Achievements.** A game with RetroAchievements shows a slim bar under its logo: a trophy (a
    badge once mastered), how many are unlocked, the points and the progress, for the game in focus
    and the one being played. Tap it to see every achievement with its badge, earned ones lit and
    first.
  - **Status** leads with the battery: the level in big figures, how long it lasts or how long until
    it's full, its temperature, and a drawn battery that charges with a soft light. Then the game
    being played, and rings for the processor, memory, the library drive and Wi-Fi.
  - **Controls** has two large sliders, brightness (the main screen, this one or both) and volume
    (the device, the menu music or the interface sounds), and tiles for Low Power, Performance, Find
    games, menu music, sounds and Screen off, which blacks the bottom screen out until you touch it.
- **Battery time** on Android, Linux, Windows and macOS: "3 h 20 min left" on battery, "Full in
  45 min" while charging, "Charged" when done. On the bottom screen and in the quick menu.
- **Move systems by touch.** Hold a system and drag it into place; the others make room, and the list
  scrolls by itself near the edges. Works on Systems and on Home's systems row. A hold let go without
  moving still opens the options, which now have "Move this system" too.
- **A Cartridge page worth opening.** Still its own tab, now one page:
  - what Cartridge is downloading right now, with its progress and how many are waiting (tap it for
    Downloads), or the last game that arrived;
  - actions as tiles: Open Cartridge, Browse, Search RomM with Fuse's keyboard, Upload, Downloads,
    Consoles and Sync;
  - your systems, to browse each on RomM, search it, or get missing firmware from RomM;
  - recently downloaded games as tiles you can play (by touch too).

Downloads: `Fuse-0.1.1-android.apk` (Android 9 or newer), `Fuse-0.1.1-x86_64.AppImage` (64-bit x86
Linux), `Fuse-0.1.1-windows-x64.msi` and `Fuse-0.1.1-windows-x64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit),
`Fuse-0.1.1-macos-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer), `Fuse-0.1.1-macos-x64.dmg` (Intel
Macs, macOS 10.15 or newer) and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. 0.1.1 installs over 0.1.0 and keeps your settings
and library.

## Changed

- **Inside a system** the header is smaller and doesn't repeat the obvious: no game count and
  emulator under the logo, no system label above the selected game, and no system name on every
  tile. More games fit from the start.
- **A system on the bottom screen** is its logo, large and centred like a game's, over its
  background. The manufacturer, game count, emulator and firmware lines are gone.
- The bottom screen's Status page no longer shows Fuse's frame rate.

## Fixed

- **Fuse in recent apps.** As the Home app, Fuse never showed in Android's recent apps (Android
  leaves the Home screen out). Fuse now runs in a normal task of its own, so it is there like any
  app, with a real preview. Home still takes you to Fuse's Home page.
- **Back after an update.** After "Restart and update", Fuse didn't open again. It now reopens once
  the new version is in, and as the Home app it always comes back after an update.
- The Windows build is back: 0.1.0's Windows package failed its own check and wasn't published.
- Android's memory reading on the bottom screen showed the wrong number.
