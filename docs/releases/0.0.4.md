# Fuse 0.0.4 - The Enhancement Update

## New

The third round from hands-on time on a dual-screen Android handheld: a phone companion, a
Storage screen, Collections with automatic series, a new keyboard, and RomM's details for the games
Cartridge downloads. Thank you for the reports, and keep them coming.

- **Phone Link.** Manage your library from a phone on the same Wi-Fi. Turn it on in Settings, Phone
  Link, set a username and password there, and scan the QR code under "Pair a phone" with your
  phone's camera. The phone shows what's playing, what Cartridge is downloading (each game with its
  progress) and art fills as they run; browses and searches your library by system; and fixes a
  game: Search as, Identify game, pick art for each slot, or fill what's missing. It can also start
  "Fill missing art", "Fill everything" or system art for all systems or one. A phone can't delete
  anything, see or change keys or passwords, or change settings. Only phones on your own network
  can connect, the password is stored as a salted hash, five wrong tries lock sign-in for a minute,
  and "Sign out all phones" (or a new password) signs every phone out. Phone Link uses port 47300,
  or the next free one, so it never clashes with Cartridge's own remote.
- **Storage.** Settings, Storage, Games and space shows each drive your library is on as a bar
  (games by system in their colours, other files, free space) and every game by what it takes,
  largest first, with a filter by system. A game's size counts all of it: every disc, cue and gdi
  tracks and whole folders. Pick games and delete their files after a confirmation that names what
  goes; save files next to a game are never counted or deleted, and only files inside the game's
  own library folder are touched. The game page shows the game's size too. On Android, deleting
  needs All files access; the screen says so and asks.
- **Collections.** A Collections view in the Library header: make your own collection, add or
  remove games with a checklist, rename it, give it art or let its games' covers make a collage,
  or delete it. Fuse also finds series on its own: games whose details name a series (IGDB, or RomM
  through Cartridge) are grouped by it, and others by a shared title start ("Super Mario", "The
  Legend of Zelda") when three or more games share it. Hide a series you don't want, or keep it as
  your own. Settings, Library turns Collections or automatic series off (nothing is deleted).
- **A keyboard that feels like a phone's.** A real text field with a blinking caret, selection and
  text that scrolls to the caret. Tap to place the caret, drag to move it, double tap to select a
  word. 123 and #+= pages, key pop-ups, holding Delete speeds up to whole words, dragging along
  Space moves the caret, and a double tap on Shift locks capitals. With a controller: X deletes, Y
  types a space, LB and RB move the caret, LT and RT jump a word, Start finishes.
- **Updates with progress.** Updating Fuse downloads in the background with its progress in
  Settings, Updates, in the Quick Menu and as a ring in the top line. Once it's ready, "Restart and
  update" installs it: the Linux app restarts into the new version, and on Android the system asks
  to confirm and starts Fuse again. A dismissed confirmation can be retried without downloading
  again.
- **Follow Cartridge's downloads in Fuse.** The Cartridge tab shows the current download with its
  percentage, system and size, then every queued game with its own progress, paused or failed.
  While Cartridge downloads, the top line shows a progress ring that opens the Cartridge tab.
- **RomM's details for games Cartridge downloaded.** With a Cartridge that has the new bridge
  ([MAtiyaaa/cartridge#30](https://github.com/MAtiyaaa/cartridge/pull/30)), games it downloaded get
  RomM's description, year, developer, publisher, genres, series, players and rating, and its cover
  and logo. They fill what's empty and replace what was found online, but never what you edited or
  picked yourself. Settings, Cartridge, "Details and art from RomM" turns it off. With Cartridge
  0.9.10 everything else works as before.
- **Turn Cartridge off.** Settings, Cartridge starts with "Cartridge support". Off, Fuse leaves
  Cartridge alone: no tab, widget, channel, Quick Menu tile or menu entries, and no status reads.
- **Fill everything.** Next to "Fill missing art" in Settings, Media and Scraping: every art kind,
  screenshots and details (now with series and rating from IGDB), plus system art.
- **Open a game's page instead of playing.** Settings, Library, "Selecting a game": play it (as
  before) or open its page.

Downloads: `Fuse-0.0.4-android.apk` (Android 9 or newer), `Fuse-0.0.4-x86_64.AppImage` (64-bit x86
Linux) and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. 0.0.4 installs over 0.0.3 and keeps your settings.

## Changed

- **Art fills are faster and show their progress.** Three games at a time (one in Low Power Mode),
  each source within its own limits, and kinds no source you set up can find (icons need a
  SteamGridDB key) are no longer asked for on every game. A bulk "Fill missing" remembers for two
  weeks what the sources didn't have, so the next run moves on; a new key, a new Search as name or
  filling one game asks again. Progress shows live in Settings and in the top line (select it to
  stop), a message says when it's done, and games with several close matches are listed as "games
  need you" and open Identify game.
- **Second screen.** Systems show their logo and the art pack's panel (a panel in the system's
  colour until the pack is downloaded) with their game count, emulator and firmware. Moving between
  items slides the details the way you moved, the background crossfades, and the logo settles in.
- **System pages.** A system's page keeps the Systems header, with the logo in the same place, and
  the system's own background or art panel while you move between its games. A game's own
  background still wins.
- **Channels Home.** A spotlight across the top tells the focused channel's story: its game's logo,
  playtime and when you played, and the covers that come next. Game channels became collages of
  their covers over the first game's background, systems show their cards, apps their icons. The
  time moved to the top line.
- **Top line tabs.** When the tabs don't all fit, they scroll like a carousel: the active tab is
  always whole, and tabs passing either edge shrink and fade.
- **Names.** List numbers and codes at the start of a file name are cleaned, with or without
  separators ("12 - ", "123.", "0123 ", "#12", "NUS-012"), while numbers that are the title stay
  (1942, 1080 Snowboarding, 007). Existing names are cleaned once (undoable), and searches always
  use the new rules.

## Fixed

- A game's written title showed for a moment before its logo appeared. Logos and art now appear
  when they're ready, without the title flashing first, and art already in memory shows without a
  fade.
- The top line cut a tab short ("Car...") when there wasn't room.
- Manage media's art preview ran off a handheld's screen. It now fits both the width and the
  height left, for covers, icons, banners, backgrounds, logos and screenshots.
- DuckStation said a CHD file (Pepsiman.chd) doesn't exist. Fuse now hands DuckStation single-file
  images (CHD, PBP, ISO and more) through its own share with a read grant, and checks that a game's
  file is still there before launching, with a message if it moved.
- The second screen only showed the clock on Flow Home: Flow never told it which game or system was
  selected.
- "Fill missing art" kept coming back to games that looked done, and took far longer than needed.
