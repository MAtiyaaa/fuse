# Fuse 0.1.3 - The Capture Update

## New

Screenshots and recordings of Fuse, from the quick menu or the controller. Android only for now.

- **Screenshots.** Fuse saves a picture of what it shows as a PNG in Pictures/Fuse, where the gallery
  finds it.
  - **From the quick menu:** select Screenshot. The menu closes and a 3, 2, 1 countdown runs in the
    middle of the screen, so the picture shows the screen you were on, not the menu.
  - **From the controller:** press L3 and R3 together (both sticks in) for a screenshot at once.
  - A soft flash and a small card in the corner show what was saved. The countdown, the flash and the
    card are never in the picture.
- **Recordings.** Fuse records its screen as an MP4 (H.264 video, AAC sound) in Movies/Fuse.
  - **From the quick menu:** hold Screenshot (hold A, or a long press). After Android's "Start
    recording?" prompt, the same 3 second countdown runs.
  - **From the controller:** hold L3 and R3 together for a moment. Recording starts after Android's
    prompt, with no countdown.
  - While it records, the top line shows "Recording 0:42" with a ring that fills towards the limit.
  - **To stop:** press L3 and R3 again, select Stop in the quick menu, select the top line's
    Recording chip, or press Stop in the notification. The card in the corner shows a frame from it.
  - **Only Fuse is recorded.** Starting a game or going Home stops and saves the recording.
  - Recordings stop by themselves after 30 minutes, or when free storage drops under 300 MB.
- **Sound in recordings.** Recordings include Fuse's music. Android asks for the microphone
  permission the first time; Fuse uses it only for Android's playback capture of its own sound and
  never records the microphone. Button sounds are never recorded (Android keeps them out). Say no,
  or turn Record sound off, and recordings are silent.
- **Settings, Inputs, Screenshots and recordings.** Turn the L3 + R3 shortcut off (the quick menu
  still works), turn Record sound on or off, and see where captures are saved.

## Changed

- The quick menu has a Screenshot tile after Bluetooth on Android; the tiles after it move along by
  one. While a recording runs, the tile reads Stop with how long it has recorded.
- The top line's activity chips keep their progress ring while they ask for attention, so the
  Recording chip shows both its dot and how far it is towards the limit.

## Fixed

- The macOS download could fail to build now and then while the build machine was still looking at
  the new disk image. Building it now tries again.
