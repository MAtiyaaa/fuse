# Fuse 0.0.5 - The Box Art Update

## New

The fourth round from hands-on time on a dual-screen Android handheld: square box art for every
game, menu music, a choice of screen for everything you start, games going up to your RomM server,
and art that finds itself. Thank you for the reports, and keep them coming.

- **Box art.** Games now show square box art (the style iisu and console home screens use) in the
  Box art layout (called Icons before), on Home, in lists, search and menus. It comes from
  SteamGridDB's square grids, from a `squares` folder or `-square` images next to your library, or
  from a file you pick. A game without box art shows its icon, then its cover drawn whole over a
  soft blur of itself, so its title is never cropped, then its wide art. Manage media and Phone Link
  have a Box art slot next to Icon, and systems can have their own box art too.
- **Menu music.** Fuse plays the album *jam channel* by boipurple under its menus: puddleworld by
  default, and alright apothecary during first-time setup, crossfading into the menu song when setup
  ends. Music stops while a game runs. Settings, Sound, "Song" picks any of the album's ten songs or
  your own file, and a "Music by boipurple" row credits the artist (so do Licences and the
  README).
- **Top or bottom screen.** On a device with two screens, starting a game or an app asks which
  screen it opens on, and whether to remember it just this time, for that game or app, or for all
  games of its system or all apps. Settings, Displays sets "Games open on" and "Apps open on" (Top
  screen, Bottom screen or Ask each time), a system's settings can have its own, and a game's or
  app's options have "Screen". Games that use both screens (DS, 3DS, Wii U) never ask.
- **Upload to RomM.** A game's options have "Upload to RomM", and the Cartridge tab has "Upload a
  game". Fuse hands the game to Cartridge with every file it has: other discs and tracks, and for
  folder games DLC and updates in their own folders. Cartridge shows what it would send and uploads
  only after you press Upload there; the Cartridge tab, a ring in the top line and a message follow
  the upload. It needs a Cartridge with the new bridge
  ([MAtiyaaa/cartridge#31](https://github.com/MAtiyaaa/cartridge/pull/31)), and RomM 5.3 or newer for
  games with several files; with an older Cartridge Fuse says so and offers to update it.
- **Art that finds itself.** Settings, Media and Scraping, "Find art by itself" (on by default): a
  few seconds after a scan, or after you save a key, Fuse looks for missing art and details on its
  own. Games it looked for recently without finding more are skipped, so it only shows up when there
  is something new to try.

Downloads: `Fuse-0.0.5-android.apk` (Android 9 or newer), `Fuse-0.0.5-x86_64.AppImage` (64-bit x86
Linux) and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. 0.0.5 installs over 0.0.4 and keeps your settings.

## Changed

- **Art fills search harder and fall back.** Each source is searched by the game's title and, while
  nothing sure turns up, by other names: a name a provider gave, the cleaned file name, the title
  without its subtitle, with "&" or "and", Roman numerals or digits, without a leading "The" or
  without accents. A source that runs out of requests (TheGamesDB's monthly allowance, a rate
  limit), rejects its key or keeps failing rests for a while and the others take over; the fill says
  which one is paused, and nothing is remembered as missing while one rests. Short rate limits are
  waited out once.
- **Every kind of art in every fill.** "Fill missing art", fills for a system and a game's "Find
  Details and Art" now include box art and screenshots, and a bulk fill counts only the games it will
  really work on.
- **Storage shows logos.** Each game in Settings, Storage has its logo, or its box art or icon, or its
  initials.
- **Cartridge tab.** Its buttons scroll sideways on narrow screens, keeping the chosen one in view,
  and it shows uploads next to downloads.

## Fixed

- Moving onto a game without its own background showed the previous game's background. It now shows
  the system's background (or its art panel).
- In Manage media, scrolling down to the last slot and back up left the top rows hidden. The header
  and every row come back.
- A pick list with square game art left a wide gap before each name.
