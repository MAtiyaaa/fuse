# Fuse 0.1.5 - The Craft Update

## New

- **Nine new themes, 19 in all.** Pitch (true black for OLED screens, nothing moves), Blossom
  (petals at dusk), Lagoon (light rippling through water), Canopy (fireflies in a quiet wood),
  Sundown (a neon sun over a grid), and four bright ones: Opal, Noon, Ridge and Olive.
  - Eight new animated backgrounds drawn in code: petals, horizon, fireflies, caustics, lcd, mesh,
    contours and dunes. Theme files can use them too (docs/THEMES.md).
- **A new theme page.** A live preview of the focused theme with its palette and traits, filters
  for All, Dark, Bright and Yours, and a theme studio to make your own theme with a controller:
  accent, background, corners, focus, motion, sound, glass and CRT, saved like any added theme.
- **Calm loading and empty states.** Skeletons instead of spinners, and empty screens that say
  what to do next, including a guided welcome on an empty Home.
- 85 new icons.

## Changed

- Focus feels more like light on an object: a softer shadow that takes the game's colour, a sweep
  across the tile and an accent bar that springs into place. Mouse hover and touch press have
  their own feedback everywhere.
- Changing the theme crossfades instead of snapping.
- Menus have icon wells, groups and a highlight that glides between rows. Dialogs scale in and
  close faster than they open. Toasts spring in and show how long they will stay.
- The top bar's tab underline glides, and Settings or Search no longer show Home as the open tab.
  Pages slide in the direction you move.
- Starting a game shows its art, system and title with an honest status line.
- Home, Library, Systems, a game's page, Search, Collections, Settings, the quick menu,
  Achievements, Apps and Phone Link on the web were all refined.
- Generated art for games without art is redesigned, and titles never break mid-word.
- Controller glyphs are redrawn for every pad style, the on-screen keyboard feels like a console
  keyboard, and the CRT effect has finer scanlines and keeps text crisp.
- Small grey text is easier to read in every theme.

## Fixed

- Interface sounds no longer click at their start or end.
- The first glyph of the hint line was clipped while hints changed.
- Games without art darkened bright themes.
