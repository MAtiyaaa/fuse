# Fuse 0.3.7.2 - The Motion Update

Setup asks about Fuse Sync before Who's playing, and the controller has a new look.

## New

- **A new controller picture.** Setup's controller test and Settings, Controls show a controller
  drawn the way Fuse looks: a solid body with a clean outline, an outlined D-pad, sticks and face
  buttons, small View and Menu buttons, and Fuse's own mark as the button in the middle. Every
  button you press fills with your theme's colour and glows.

## Changed

- **Fuse Sync comes before Who's playing in setup.** Setup now goes: Welcome, Tuned for this
  device, Home screen, Fuse Sync, Who's playing, Let Fuse see your games, Where are your games,
  emulators found, BIOS check, and the rest. Signing in to Fuse Sync first means Who's playing shows
  your household's profiles to choose from; without Fuse Sync, it starts with none.

## Fixed

- **Who's playing came before signing in.** It asked you to make a profile before you could sign in
  to the household that already had yours.
