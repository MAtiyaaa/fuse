# Fuse 0.3.7.1 - The Motion Update

System cards on a handheld are back to the size they were.

## New

- Nothing new in this release: it puts one thing back as it was.

## Changed

- **Systems page on a handheld.** On a handheld's screen, such as the AYN Thor's, the Systems page
  shows its system cards at the size they had before 0.3.6.5 (five across the Thor's upper screen)
  instead of three large ones. TVs and other large screens keep the size they have now, and the
  games inside each system are unchanged.

## Fixed

- **System cards were much bigger on a handheld.** Since the Systems page became arrangeable like
  Home (0.3.6.5), its cards were sized for a TV everywhere, so a handheld showed only three large
  cards across. A small screen now gets the grid it always had.
