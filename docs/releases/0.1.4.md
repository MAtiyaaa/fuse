# Fuse 0.1.4 - The Gallery Update

## New

- **Your screenshots and recordings on your phone.** Phone Link has a Captures tab with everything
  Fuse saved on the device, grouped by day, newest first.
  - Filter by All, Screenshots or Recordings.
  - Tap one to look at it or play it. Swipe (or use the arrows) for the next one; the date, size
    and resolution are underneath.
  - Download it from there, straight to your phone.
  - Select (or a long press) to choose several, then download them together as one zip.
  - New captures show up by themselves while the tab is open.
  - Download links are made for your signed-in phone and stop working after ten minutes.
    Phone Link still can't delete anything, so captures are only removed on the device.
  - The tab is there on devices that take captures (Android for now).
- **The bottom screen stays with you.** On two-screen devices, Fuse's bottom screen now stays when
  you start a game or open an app, instead of going blank.
  - While a game runs, it shows that game with its playtime and achievements, in either second
    screen mode.
  - It never takes the controller from the game.
  - DS, DSi, 3DS and Wii U games, and anything you open on the bottom screen, still get the whole
    screen; Fuse comes back there when you return.
  - Want the bottom screen for something else? Controls, Hide frees it until you're back in Fuse.

## Changed

- Away from Fuse, the bottom screen no longer keeps the device awake: the screen turns off as
  usual when nothing is playing.
- Settings, Displays, Second screen: "Show the selected game" now also shows the game you're
  playing, and the note about two-screen games says the companion stays for everything else.
- Settings, Second screen status says when Android hides the companion by itself.

## Fixed

- A system dragged to a new place in Systems could go back to where it was. The first move after
  opening Systems worked, but later ones could put a system back, move a different one, or not be
  saved. Every drag now lands where you drop it, and so does moving with the D-pad. Home's shelves
  and systems row and the Channels board had the same problem and are fixed too.
- A moved system now shows in its new place at once, even while Fuse is busy filling art.
- A system held near the top or bottom while the list scrolled by itself could snap back when let
  go.
