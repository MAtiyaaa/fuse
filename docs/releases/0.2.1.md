# Fuse 0.2.1 - The Store & Patch Update

## New

- **Addons and the Store (Android).** The Cartridge tab is now **Addons**, with Cartridge and a new
  **Store** side by side. Everywhere else Cartridge stays exactly as it was.
  - The Store lists the emulators and gaming apps of the
    [Obtainium Emulation Pack](https://github.com/RJNY/Obtainium-Emulation-Pack). On first use you
    choose the **Standard** or **Dual-Screen** edition (the one that suits your device is marked);
    you can switch in Settings, Store.
  - Install, update and uninstall from Fuse. Downloads come only from each app's own source, over
    HTTPS, are checked to be the app the Store lists, and go to Android's installer, which asks you
    every time. Fuse never installs or removes anything by itself.
  - Shelves for updates, apps for systems in your library that have no emulator yet, what is
    installed, and every category of the pack. Each app has a page with its versions, source,
    systems and release notes, and one button that follows what can be done now.
  - Downloads carry on while you use the rest of Fuse, with their progress in the top line.
    "Update all" queues every update.
  - An update is only shown when the versions really compare, never guessed. Apps the pack only
    follows, and apps that come as a zip, link to their download page instead.
  - The catalogue is kept, so the Store opens offline. A GitHub token (optional, in Settings) raises
    GitHub's limit for update checks.
- **Arrange Home freely.** Widgets now have their own place on the grid, so moving or resizing one
  never reshuffles the others.
  - Editing shows the grid. Dragging previews where the widget lands, and the widgets in the way
    move aside as you go.
  - Resize from any edge or the corner; the size snaps to the grid, and directions that can't grow
    or shrink are dimmed.
  - With a controller: A picks a widget up and the D-pad carries it. Hold X and use the D-pad to
    resize: Right and Down grow it, Left and Up shrink it. A quick press of X still opens Options.

## Changed

- **Widgets redesigned.** Continue playing shows only the game you are playing, with its logo,
  art and system. Systems shows your systems' art and logos. Favourites, Collections and Cartridge
  have their own marks.
- **The Cartridge page** is rebuilt: the download in progress up front with its system's art, the
  ways into Cartridge as cards, your systems on RomM and what just arrived. Before Cartridge is
  installed it explains itself in three steps.
- **Cartridge in its own brand.** Fuse shows Cartridge with Cartridge's own mark and colours,
  orange on near-black, and with its installed icon where Android has it.

## Fixed

- Menu music could stop after the volume was changed and stay silent until music was turned off and
  on again. It now always recovers by itself.
- The New collection card showed a dark box inside it when hovered or focused.
- Widgets jumped to other places when one was moved or resized.
