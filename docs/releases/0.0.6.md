# Fuse 0.0.6 - The Android Games Update

## New

The fifth round from hands-on time on a dual-screen Android handheld: Android games in their own
system, a way to move a game to the right system, adding a game from anywhere, Fuse in recent apps,
art that is ready before you get to it, and matching that finds games however their files are
named. Thank you for the reports, and keep them coming.

- **Android games.** Every app's options have "Type": Game, App or Emulator. Fuse decides by itself
  first (emulators it detected, then what the app says about itself), and your choice always wins.
  Games join the Android system with their icon, then box art, covers, backgrounds and logos from
  your art sources, details and play time, like any other game. They always open as their app. Apps
  has an Emulators filter next to Games.
- **A game's system.** A game's options have "System", for a game that landed in the wrong one. Fuse
  keeps your choice when it rescans, and lists the system its folder says first, then the ones that
  take its kind of file. An Android game can go back to being an app from there, or with "Not a
  game".
- **Add a game.** Settings, Library, "Add a game":
  - an app on this device, which becomes a game;
  - an APK file, which Android installs after you confirm, then it joins your games (the file stays
    where it is);
  - or a game file from anywhere on your device, for the system you pick.

  Files are picked in Fuse's own file picker, made for a controller: your storage places, then
  folders first, B goes up a folder.
- **Fuse in recent apps.** While Fuse is the Home app, it now shows in Android's recent apps right
  behind every game or app it starts, so the app switcher (or the quick-switch gesture) takes you
  straight back.

Downloads: `Fuse-0.0.6-android.apk` (Android 9 or newer), `Fuse-0.0.6-x86_64.AppImage` (64-bit x86
Linux) and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. 0.0.6 installs over 0.0.5 and keeps your settings and library; the
library moves to its new database version by itself.

## Changed

- **Top or bottom screen.** The question is a picture now: the top screen on the left and the bottom
  screen on the right, each on a little dual-screen handheld with the game's art lit on that screen.
  Two ticks below keep the answer, "Always for this game" or "Always for" its system (for apps: this
  app, or all apps). Leave both unticked for just this time.
- **Art is ready before you get to it.** A moment after Fuse starts, it gets every system's art and
  every game's art ready at the size it is drawn, Home's first, then each system in order, and
  downloads the rest ahead. Opening a system shows its box art, covers, wide capsules, logos and
  backgrounds at once, and the backgrounds of the games next to the one you're on are ready too. It
  does it again after a fill finds new art. Less in Low Power Mode.
- **Matching.** An exact name wins clearly now: "Dredge" finds DREDGE at 95% and puts Dredgers at
  23%. Words are compared whole (a typo is forgiven, an extra ending isn't), edition words count less
  and a different sequel number counts strongly against. Files named in No-Intro, Redump, GoodTools,
  TOSEC, scene or GOG style, with serials or title ids at the start, in the middle or at the end, list
  numbers, regions, versions or sizes are searched by their real name, then by other spellings and
  keywords when nothing sure turns up. Identify game searches those too.
- **Screenshots are extras.** A game counts as missing art only without box art, an icon, a cover, a
  wide capsule, a background or a logo. Screenshots still come along when a source has them, but they
  never make Fuse search again or ask you to pick.

## Fixed

- Pick lists in dialogs (a system, an emulator, a collection) no longer leave an empty band under
  their rows.
- The Android system said "No emulator installed", although Android games need none.
