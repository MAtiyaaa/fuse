# Fuse 0.1.0 - The Showcase Update

## New

The first 0.1 release. Fuse now runs on Windows and macOS as well as Android and Linux, the bottom
screen of a dual-screen handheld has pages of its own, new games get their art the moment they
arrive, and a long list of rough edges from hands-on time is gone. Thank you for every report.

- **Windows and macOS.** The full launcher, the same as on Linux:
  - Emulators are found where people keep them: portable folders on any drive, Program Files,
    AppData, the ES-DE, RetroBat, EmuDeck and LaunchBox layouts, Scoop, Chocolatey, winget and Steam
    libraries on Windows; apps in Applications (and folders inside it), Downloads and Homebrew on a
    Mac. Commands come from ES-DE's Windows and macOS rules.
  - If Fuse missed one, Settings, Emulators, "Locate an emulator" shows it where it is, in Fuse's own
    controller file picker. Choosing an emulator Fuse didn't find for a system or a game does the
    same. "Emulator folders" adds folders to search.
  - PC games: `.exe` games run from their own folder, and `.lnk` shortcuts and `.bat` scripts open
    the way Explorer opens them.
  - Controllers through SDL, the library most emulators use. Credentials are kept with Windows'
    own protection (DPAPI) or in the macOS Keychain. Start at sign-in, settings pages, battery and
    Wi-Fi follow each system.
  - Apps isn't there on any computer, Linux included: Fuse manages games there. Cartridge stays on
    Android and Linux.
- **The bottom screen has pages.** Swipe, or tap the dots:
  - The first is what it was, with a new look for games: the game's logo large and centred over its
    background (its title when it has no logo). Collections show there too, with a collage of their
    games.
  - Status: battery, processor, memory, Fuse's frame rate, Wi-Fi, storage and how long you've been
    playing.
  - Controls: brightness for the top screen, the bottom screen or both, the device volume, menu
    music and interface sounds.

  The Wi-Fi, battery and clock stay at the top of every page, and Back no longer does anything when
  pressed on the bottom screen.
- **New games are filled at once.** A game Cartridge downloads, one you add, or one a scan finds is
  identified and gets its art and details straight away, ahead of anything else Fuse is filling.
- **Art by name.** Manage media finds box art, logos and the rest by the game's current name without
  identifying it first. The best match is shown with "Not this game? Identify it" when it's wrong.
- **Achievements on more systems.** Nintendo DS, PlayStation, PlayStation 2 and PSP games are hashed
  the way RetroAchievements does, zipped ROMs are read inside the zip, and a game that can't be
  hashed (a compressed disc image) is matched by name, with a note that unlocking needs a supported
  ROM version.
- **Find games** in the quick menu looks for new games in your folders, and "Rescan every folder"
  reads them all again.
- **Credits.** Cartridge is abdu2304's work: the README, Settings, About and the licences now say so
  and link [abdu2304/cartridge](https://github.com/abdu2304/cartridge).

Downloads: `Fuse-0.1.0-android.apk` (Android 9 or newer), `Fuse-0.1.0-x86_64.AppImage` (64-bit x86
Linux), `Fuse-0.1.0-windows-x64.msi` and `Fuse-0.1.0-windows-x64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit),
`Fuse-0.1.0-macos-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer), `Fuse-0.1.0-macos-x64.dmg` (Intel
Macs, macOS 10.15 or newer) and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The APK is published first; the computer builds
join the release as they finish. 0.1.0 installs over 0.0.6 and keeps your settings and library.

The Windows and macOS builds aren't signed by Microsoft or Apple yet. On Windows, choose More info,
Run anyway the first time. On a Mac, right-click Fuse and choose Open (or allow it in System
Settings, Privacy and Security).

## Changed

- **The library's top bar.** All, Favourites and Recently played are tabs with a sliding underline;
  Collections, All systems, Sort and View are a row of compact buttons on the right. Nothing is cut
  off at the edges any more.
- **A system page folds as you scroll.** Going down, the system's logo slides away and the game's
  logo moves up and shrinks, so more games fit; it comes back at the top.
- **Collections** are system-sized tiles instead of very large ones.
- **Cartridge scrolls as one page**, recently downloaded games included.
- **Every game has a background:** its own, a screenshot, its system's, its wide art, or its box art
  softly blurred. Backgrounds load at a size that shows at once and are ready before you reach them.
- **Apps** has Pinned, Emulators and All apps (All apps first; Settings, Home can change that). The
  tab remembers which list and which app you were on. Games you moved to the Android system aren't
  listed there any more.
- **The quick menu's tiles** keep their on and off colours while selected, and Low Power says "On" or
  "Off", so you can see what a press did.
- **Updates** are announced once the release has your device's build. Windows and macOS open the
  release page to get it.
- The Storage screen's drives follow the selected game, for screens without touch.

## Fixed

- Choosing box art asked "Which game is this?" every time, even after you answered.
- A game without a background, or one still loading, showed the previous game's background. Leaving
  an app in Apps kept its background on other tabs.
- Opening a system (Switch, for example) started on its fifth game instead of the first.
- On Home's Channels, going down from the row of three skipped the row of two below it.
- "Open Cartridge" and the first library tab were cut off at the left edge.
- Settings returns to the row you were on after a screen it opened, like a file picker.
